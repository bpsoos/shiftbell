# Domain glossary

## Chore

One executable instance of work with its own deadline and a snapshot of its name and description. A chore is either one-off or scheduled.

## Active chore

A chore that has not been completed.

## Completed chore

A chore with a recorded completion date.

## One-off chore

A chore that is independent of a schedule.

## Scheduled chore

A chore belonging to a schedule. Completing it creates its successor.

## Chore template

A reusable name and description used to create chores and schedules.

## Schedule

A recurring plan associated with one chore template and an interval. An active schedule has at most one active chore and may have many completed chores.

## Successor

The next active chore in a schedule, based on the completed chore's completion date and the schedule's interval.

## Permanent deactivation

An irreversible end to a schedule or chore template that preserves its history.
